# Third-party datasets

This repository does not contain GeoLife, T-Drive, Porto, or any other external trajectory corpus.

GeoLife is distributed by Microsoft Research under its own research license, which does not allow redistribution of the traces. The Supplement summarizes twenty pairs drawn from eight GeoLife users. Those pairs are not shared vehicles, and the traces are not included here. Obtain the corpus from the Microsoft Research GeoLife project page and cite the user guide required by that license. This repository does not supply page numbers for that guide.

T-Drive and the Porto taxi traces identify taxis or devices. They are not two drivers of one shared vehicle. Do not treat a local copy of either corpus as part of this release.

The Supplement's GeoLife numbers were computed outside this repository. They cannot be regenerated from the derived archive.
